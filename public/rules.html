House Rules

A smoother stay for everyone. Please review these guidelines before confirming your booking.

Check-in & check-out

  • Check-in: 12:30 PM. Check-out: 12:00 PM.
  • Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. Extra charges may apply for late check-out if not arranged in advance.

Care for the apartment

  • No smoking inside the apartment. An ashtray is provided for outdoor use.
  • No pets are allowed.
  • Do not rearrange furniture or put up decorations inside the apartment.
  • Keep noise levels low to avoid disturbing other residents.

Guest responsibilities

  • Guests are responsible for damage to the property or furniture.
  • Fines for damage or breaches of the rules vary depending on the damage.
  • Take all personal belongings when leaving. Management is not responsible for items left after check-out.
  • Turn off the electricity breakers before leaving the apartment.

Identification & conduct

  • The main guest’s ID is held by staff until check-out and returned after outstanding dues and fines are cleared.
  • Staff may check the identification of any guest inside the property. A booking may be cancelled if guests fail to provide ID.
  • Illegal activities, alcohol, drugs and weapons are prohibited under the house rules.
  • Violations may result in cancellation and guests being required to vacate immediately.
  • If check-out time has passed and the guest is not responding to calls or messages, management may enter to proceed with check-out.

Cancellation & refunds

  • Bookings of two days or more cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • Notify us at least 48 hours before your booking date to request cancellation, a refund or a date change. Please confirm eligibility with us before payment.
  • Same-day and on-the-spot bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • Eligible cancellation refunds are returned to the guest’s account within 24 hours.

Before you book
